### Full description — ~3,240 / 4,000

```
Hisn is a quiet, offline companion for the remembrances of the day — the morning
and evening adhkar, the duas for the small moments, the Mushaf, the tasbih, the
prayer times, and the qibla. No account, no advertising, no tracking, and no
network needed once it is installed.

ADHKAR AND DUAS
• Morning, evening, after prayer, before sleep, on waking, and everyday life.
• Every dua carries its Arabic, transliteration, meaning, repetition count and
  its source reference — Qur'an, Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, an-Nasa'i.
• Tap a dua to count its repetitions down; a progress rule tracks the whole set.
• A time-aware pointer on the home screen sends you to the set that belongs to
  this part of the day, following the prayer periods rather than the clock.
• Write and save your own duas alongside the bundled ones.

THE QUR'AN
• The Madani Mushaf page by page in the King Fahd (QCF v4) page fonts, swiped
  right to left like the printed copy, with go-to-verse and pinch to full screen.
• The verses printed on the page in view are listed beside it as text, each with
  its citation, so a particular ayah can be read, copied, saved or shared.
• Meanings in your language: The Noble Qur'an (Hilali & Khan) in English, the
  Kementerian Agama rendering in Indonesian, and at-Tafsir al-Muyassar in Arabic.
• Bookmarks keep an ayah worth returning to apart from the page you stopped at.

PRAYER TIMES AND QIBLA
• The five daily times for your location, with a live countdown to the next.
• Choose the calculation method and the Asr rule; use GPS or pick a city.
• Optional reminders at each prayer, with a per-prayer iqamah delay so the
  reminder can arrive a few minutes after the adhan, as at your mosque.
• A qibla compass corrected from magnetic to true north with the World Magnetic
  Model — and one that tells you when your phone is tilted, uncalibrated, or
  near metal instead of showing a confident needle pointing the wrong way.

TASBIH
• Tap-anywhere counter with presets, an animated ring, set tracking and haptics.
• Counts persist, and the home-screen widget counts into the same total.

AND ALSO
• Home-screen widgets: prayer times, next prayer countdown, tasbih, the adhkar
  of the moment, and a verse for the day.
• Sunnah fasts and the occasions of the Hijri year, each with its reference, and
  the days on which fasting is forbidden marked as such.
• Share a dua or a verse as a card set in the app's own hand — the source always
  travels with it.
• Full-text search across every dua, in Arabic or in translation.
• Six paper-and-ink palettes, light and dark, adjustable text size, and English,
  Arabic and Indonesian interfaces.
• Back up everything the app knows to a single readable file, and restore it.

PRIVACY
Hisn has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. What you save
stays on your device. Location, if you grant it, is used on the device to work
out your prayer times and qibla; the only time it leaves is when your phone's
own operating system resolves it into a place name to show you.

Prayer times and qibla direction are calculated estimates and depend on your
settings, your GPS fix and your device's compass. Verify them against your local
mosque before relying on them.
```

## Notes on the copy

- **No keyword stuffing.** Play's metadata policy treats repeated keyword lists
  as spam and it is a common cause of listing rejection. Every term above
  appears because it names something the app does.
- **The disclaimer paragraph is deliberate.** Prayer times and qibla are
  calculated estimates; saying so in the listing matches `TERMS.md` §2 and
  removes the one review complaint you cannot answer.
- **No feature is promised that the build does not have.** Hands-free adhkar
  recitation is held back (`kAdhkarAudioEnabled`) and is therefore absent from
  all three descriptions. Add it here in the same change that enables it.
- **Do not translate the app name into Arabic script alone** — `حصن` reads
  correctly to an Arabic speaker searching, and the Latin `Hisn` is what an
  English or Indonesian speaker types. Each listing above already does this.
