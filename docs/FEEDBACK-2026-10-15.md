## Content provenance

Exact reviewed changes and primary-source URLs are recorded in
`tool/feedback/*updates.json`. These proposals are merged by
`tool/feedback/apply_updates.py`; the Arabic overlay generator preserves their
instructions and references on regeneration.

The Urdu Quran edition is Muhammad Junagarhi (`ur.junagarhi`) from
[Tanzil](https://tanzil.net/trans/). The generator validates every surah and verse
identifier before writing the 114 surahs / 6,236 meanings. Tanzil's existing
non-commercial conditions still apply, and attribution travels with meanings.

Urdu hadith-dua meanings are original project draft renderings of the cited
Arabic. They do not claim to reproduce a published Urdu translator's edition;
qualified Urdu/religious review remains a release acceptance step, like the
project's other draft religious content.

New interface strings cover English, Arabic, Indonesian and Urdu. Backup and
restore include guide completion and user preferences, without carrying native
rule IDs or permission grants. App resume reconciles changes made in Android
settings. Neither app launch nor the guide requests these new permissions.

Wudu sources: [Muslim 234b](https://sunnah.com/muslim:234b) for the complete
testimony, [Muslim 234a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:234a) for the eight gates
virtue after properly completed wudu. Waking action:
[Bukhari 183](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:183). Arabic and Urdu overlay generators
remain byte-for-byte reproducible.

Silence activation is refused unless its finite end and an independent backup
check are armed. Overlapping windows, midnight, stale alarms and skipping the
current period are handled natively. Hisn's own adhan playback defers silence
until playback finishes, keeping the original end deadline. Schedules replenish
while Flutter is closed and recover on boot and clock/timezone changes.

Android owns delivery timing. Revoking precise-alarm access cancels exact
alarms; the independent inexact backup can be delayed by the OS. The settings
screen states this limitation. Android 7–9's condition-provider path fails
closed if the OS will not connect it. Real devices, including OEM battery
restrictions, remain an acceptance step. No Android test device was connected.
