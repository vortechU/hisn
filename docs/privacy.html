Privacy Policy — Hisn

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Hisn is a dua, adhkar, Quran, Tasbih, prayer-times, and Qibla companion app. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: the developer of Hisn does not collect, store, or receive any personal data about you. Hisn has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no AI services, no crash reporting, and no backend server. Everything the app needs to work runs on your device.

We collect no personal data

Hisn does not ask for, and the developer never receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials (there are no accounts);
  • usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data;
  • any content you create in the app.

Anything you save in Hisn — favorites, custom duas you write, settings, streaks, and Quran bookmarks — is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Location

Hisn can use your device's precise (GPS) location for exactly two features:

  1. Prayer-time calculation — to compute the five daily prayer times for where you are.
  2. Qibla direction — to determine the bearing to the Kaaba from your position.

Location is optional: you can instead pick a city manually and never grant location access.

Where your location goes:

  • Your coordinates are used on your device for the calculations above and are stored only on your device (in the app's local settings). They are never sent to any server operated by the developer — the developer has no server.
  • To show a readable place name (for example, "Madinah") next to your prayer times, Hisn passes your coordinates to your device operating system's geocoding service — Google Play Services on most Android devices, or Apple's location services on iOS — purely to resolve that name. This is the only situation in which your coordinates leave the device, and they go to the operating-system vendor, not to the developer. That processing is governed by their privacy policies:
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We do not sell, share, or track

Hisn does not sell your data, does not share it with third parties for their own purposes, and does not use any data to track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising or profiling of any kind.

Children's privacy

Hisn does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 16. Because no personal data is collected, no special parental consent is required to use it. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal data through the app, please contact us — though, by design, the app gives the developer no way to receive such data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date.
