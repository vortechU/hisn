# Urdu dua content provenance

`assets/data/duas.ur.json` is generated by `tool/gen_ur_dua_overlay.py`.

The hadith meanings, titles, virtues and action guidance are **original project
Urdu drafts** rendered from the existing bundled Arabic/English dua content.
They require review by a qualified Urdu reader and a qualified religious
reviewer before release. Automated coverage checks establish completeness and
prevent English fallback; they do not establish translation or religious
acceptance. Hadith reference numbers and grading notes follow the current base
content. This work does not independently reauthenticate those narrations.

The official Osoul Urdu edition of *Hisn al-Muslim* was inspected for source
feasibility. Its PDF text extraction is unreliable, and republication rights
for its Urdu wording were not established. No wording from that edition was
copied, and its translator is not credited for the project's draft prose.

Quran meanings are copied verbatim from the project's bundled **Muhammad
Junagarhi**, `ur.junagarhi`, translation downloaded from
[Tanzil](https://tanzil.net/trans/ur.junagarhi). The edition, credit, source and
[Tanzil translation terms](https://tanzil.net/trans/) remain recorded in the
overlay metadata and the Quran translation assets. Follow the existing Quran
translation license terms, including noncommercial use and translator credit.

The four Ayat al-Kursi cards use the complete meaning of 2:255. The twelve
three-surah cards use the complete meanings of 112–114 preceded by the exact
basmalah translation from 1:1. The waking card uses all eleven meanings of
3:190–200. Dua fragments from 2:286, 3:16, 3:173, 7:23, 9:129 and 21:87 are
exact suffix excerpts matching the Arabic prayer on the card, with the
surrounding narrative omitted. The mixed travel and calamity prayers retain
exact Quran excerpts of 43:13–14 and 2:156 alongside separately authored hadith
prose. Formatting joins passages and verse numbers without editing the
translator's characters inside each passage.

Regenerate after base-content changes:

```powershell
python -X utf8 tool/gen_ur_dua_overlay.py
```

Generation fails when a required meaning, virtue or instruction is missing;
every shipped entry has an Urdu title, meaning and source. The generator never
fills a missing field with English. The application should retain its draft
review disclosure until human review is complete.
